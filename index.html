Tanveer Hussain (born July 10, 2000), professionally known as VexorNull, is a Pakistani software developer, web architect, and open-source contributor based in Karachi, Pakistan. He is best known for creating dynamic web applications, lightweight UI/UX frameworks, real-time cloud-integrated portals, and system optimization scripts.

1. Career & Expertise

Tanveer Hussain operates under the online identity @VexorNull across various digital platforms including GitHub, YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.
